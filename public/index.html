Trading Masterclass & Rules
Risk management, confluence & scoring guide
1. The 1% - 2% Risk Rule
Never risk more than 1.5% of your total equity on any single trade setup. At 1.5% risk, it takes over 20 consecutive losses to experience a severe 30% drawdown. Use the Trade Score engine to calculate position size derived strictly from your stop loss distance in points or pips, never from fixed lot habits.
2. Confluence Stacking & Edge
A high-probability edge requires at least 3 distinct confluence factors aligning at the same price location: (1) Higher Timeframe Trend Alignment, (2) Fresh Supply/Demand or Order Block Zone, and (3) Confirmed Trigger Rejection Candle. When confluence stacks to 80+ points, win probability increases significantly.
3. Overcoming FOMO & Revenge Trading
The market presents thousands of opportunities every month. Missing a move has zero negative impact on your account balance. If a trade does not satisfy your pre-flight checklist with at least 60 points, passing on the trade IS a win because it protects your mental clarity and financial capital.
4. Understanding the 0 - 100 Score
80 - 100 (A+ Quality): All core technical and risk conditions satisfied. Full planned risk position allowed.

60 - 79 (Moderate Quality): Minor confluences missing. Half position risk recommended (0.75%).

< 60 (Weak / Avoid): Lacks edge or violates risk parameters. Strictly avoid execution.
